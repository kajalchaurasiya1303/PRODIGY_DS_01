# PRODIGY_DS_01 – Data Visualization

## Prodigy InfoTech Data Science Internship

### Task 01

### Objective
Create a bar chart or histogram to visualize the distribution of a continuous or categorical variable, such as population, age, or gender.

## Dataset
The dataset used for this task is **Population of India.csv**.

It contains population information for different States and Union Territories of India, including male and female population.

## Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Task Performed
In this task, the population data was analyzed and visualized using a bar chart.

A grouped bar chart was created to compare the **Male and Female population** of different States/Union Territories.

## Visualization
The bar chart helps to easily compare the male and female population across different States and Union Territories.

## Conclusion
The visualization provides a clear comparison of male and female population across different States and Union Territories. It makes the population data easier to understand and identify differences between male and female populations.
